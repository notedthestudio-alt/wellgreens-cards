Wellgreens · Product cards v5 · Vista

Seamless photos, real deal badges.

Products are cut out and set on one consistent tile, deals sit in a corner ribbon, and shoppers can switch every weight between grams and ounces.

Tile
  1. Search first. The bar cycles through suggestions. Tap it to see popular searches, then type to filter live.
  2. Tap + on a photo. It turns into a quantity stepper and the item glides into the cart.
  3. Switch sizes. The weight pill and price roll to the new value and the selector slides over.
  4. Tap g / oz in the header. Every pill, picker and price-per rolls between grams and ounces.
  5. Watch the cart bar. It shows savings and fills a progress bar toward the deal.
  6. Best value sorts by price per gram and reveals that number on each card, only when you ask for it.

Prototype. Prices are from the Vista menu on Nov 10, 2026. Deal and savings math is illustrative; checkout calculates the real totals.
